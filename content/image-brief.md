# Image brief

Hero images for FrenchVineyard.com, a travel guide to French wine regions. A hero runs full-bleed behind a white headline with a dark fade at the bottom, and is cropped to anything from 16:9 on a laptop to roughly 4:5 on a phone.

## What a good hero looks like

- **Warm light.** Early morning or late afternoon sun, golden or soft. Overcast is acceptable when the subject is strong; harsh midday glare, flat grey and night shots are not.
- **Low horizon.** The horizon sits in the lower third, or the frame is filled with rows, slopes or stone, so the headline has calm sky or texture above it and the subject survives a centered crop.
- **Vineyard or cellar subject.** Vine rows, hillsides of vines, a wine village among its vineyards, chalk or stone cellars, barrels in a cellar, a château seen across its vines, harvest work in the rows. For a town page, the town itself among or beside its vineyards is best; a recognizable street or square is acceptable.
- **Sense of place.** The landscape should look like the page's region: chalk slopes and villages for Champagne, flat gravel and grand châteaux for the Médoc, limestone slopes and walled clos for Burgundy.
- **Clean and calm.** One clear subject, little clutter, no strong color casts, sharp at the subject, at least 1600 px wide, landscape.

## Hard rejects (score 0)

- People drinking, glasses raised, toasts, parties, crowds celebrating, or anyone visibly drunk.
- Glasses of wine as the main subject, bottles as the main subject, wine being poured.
- Logos, brand names or readable labels on bottles, barrels, signs or vehicles; watermarks; captions or text overlays.
- Advertising or anything that sells wine (price tags, shop shelves, promotions).
- Collages, frames, heavy filters, HDR halos, tilted horizons, fisheye, illustrations or renders presented as photos.
- Identifiable faces in close-up.

## Weaker but usable

- Cityscapes without vines (fine for a city page, weak for a vineyard topic).
- A cellar with barrels whose stencilled marks are unreadable.
- Winter vines, mist, harvest machinery.

## For generated images (only for generic topics that name no real place)

A photographic look of a generic French vineyard or cellar scene that matches the brief above: warm low sun, low horizon, vine rows or a chalk cellar, no people drinking, no glasses, no bottles in focus, no text, no logos, no recognizable landmark or building. Never depict a real, named place.
