# How we rank tours

This file is the source for the "How we ranked these" box on ranked-list pages. The drafting step summarizes it; it does not choose or rank tours. Every tour on the site is picked and ordered by hand by the site's editor in the Tours tab. **Editor: keep this file true to how you actually choose. Readers see a summary of it.**

## What a tour needs to be considered

- It visits working wine estates or cellars in the region, not just a city walk with a tasting at the end.
- **Rating:** at least 4.5 from at least 50 reviews. Day trips from Paris need at least 100 reviews.
- **Free cancellation** is offered.
- **An English-speaking guide** leads the tour.
- **The group size is stated.** Groups under 16 are preferred, and private tours count as small.
- **A clear meeting point or a pickup** is given.
- **One option per price tier in each category of a region:** under €60, €60 to €150, and over €150. Each region's category lists at most one tour per tier, so readers see a real choice of budgets, not three versions of the same day.
- **The same tour on two sites:** when GetYourGuide and Viator sell the same tour, the listing with more reviews is the one shown.

## How the order is set

1. **Fit for the page.** A tour that matches the page's town and type of trip ranks above one that only passes through.
2. **What's included.** Transport from the meeting point, the number of estates visited and whether tastings are included in the price.
3. **Ratings and review volume**, as shown on the provider's page on the date in the tour's `last_checked`.
4. **Price for what you get.** Lower price breaks ties; it never lifts a weaker tour above a stronger one.

## What doesn't affect the order

- Commission. The site earns a commission from some providers, and the rate is not a ranking factor.
- Operators cannot pay to be listed or to move up.

## Upkeep

The editor rechecks every listed tour's price, rating and availability at least every six months and removes tours that no longer meet the rules above.
