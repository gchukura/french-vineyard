# Template: practical

A practical page answers one logistics question, such as "Paris to Reims by train" or "Do you need a car in Burgundy?" Readers often arrive from search on a phone, so the answer comes first.

## Structure

1. **Opening (no heading), 1 paragraph.** Restate the question in plain words with the target keyword.
2. **## The short answer.** Two to four sentences that fully answer the question with the key numbers from the fact sheet.
3. **## Your options.** One `<PracticalTable>` comparing the options (for example `Option|From|Time|Cost`), then a short paragraph per option.
4. **## Before you go.** The relevant warnings from the fact sheet as a short list.
5. If the brief has tours that solve the problem (for example, a day tour with transport from Paris), one sentence and `<TourCards ids="…" />` at the end of **Your options**.

## Length

500–1,200 words in the body.
