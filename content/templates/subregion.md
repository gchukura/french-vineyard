# Template: subregion

A wine area page covers one part of a region, such as the Côte des Blancs or the Médoc. The page template already shows, around your text: a table of the area's appellation facts, the estates to visit from our Estates tab, tours, and the towns in the area. So the body must **not** include a table, list estates as a directory or embed cards.

The `standfirst` you return becomes the first paragraph of the page. Make it two sentences that say where the area is and what sets its wine apart.

## Structure

1. **## The wine.** What grows here and why it tastes different from the rest of the region (soil, slope, grape, classification), using only the fact sheet. Two or three short paragraphs.
2. **## Visiting.** How a visit to this area works: which villages to base yourself in, whether estates take walk-ins or need appointments, the fee range with USD in parentheses, and up to three named estates from the fact sheet as examples. End with the most useful of the fact sheet's warnings.

## Length

350–800 words in the body. There is no FAQ on wine area pages; return the `faq` field anyway (it is not shown).
